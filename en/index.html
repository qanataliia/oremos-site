Verse of the Day

A verse to keep in your heart

Each day, a Bible passage with a warm reflection written for everyday life, and one concrete thing to live it out.

  • The full passage, not just a single line.
  • “For today”: one simple thing to do during the day.
  • Share the verse with someone who needs to hear it.

Daily routine

A small rhythm, every day

No need to decide what to pray: Oremos gets each day ready for you.

Morning: the Verse

Verse, Reflection and Prayer, in about 5 minutes. Home shows what you’ve done today.

During the day: the Rosary

Today’s mysteries, guided bead by bead, whenever you have a moment.

Night: the Daily Examen

Look back on your day with God, in the way of Saint Ignatius: give thanks, ask for light and ask forgiveness.

Pick a time for your daily reminder and watch your tree grow with every day of prayer.

Guided Rosary

The Rosary that already knows today’s mysteries

Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious or Luminous: Oremos opens today’s mysteries and walks with you bead by bead.

  • A Bible passage and a grace to ask for in each mystery.
  • Every prayer on screen, for anyone who doesn’t know them by heart.
  • The beads move with you, so you never lose your place.

Saints

Learn from those who followed Jesus

The lives of saints like Saint Padre Pio, the shepherd children of Fatima, Saint Anthony, Saint Carlo Acutis and Saint Thérèse, told simply.

  • Their life, in a few paragraphs.
  • A prayer to pray with each saint.
  • Something for today, inspired by their example.

Confession

Prepare for confession calmly

After the Daily Examen, note what you want to bring to the sacrament. When you go to confession, it’s all there.

  • Only on this phone: the list never goes online.
  • Protected: only someone who can unlock the phone can open it.
  • Deleted after you go to confession.
  • A step-by-step guide, on a dark, discreet screen.

What is Oremos?

A Catholic app for daily prayer

Oremos is a free app for daily prayer. It includes the Verse of the Day with a reflection, a daily routine with the Daily Examen at night, the guided Rosary with each day’s mysteries (Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious and Luminous), the lives of the saints and private help preparing for confession. It works without an account and offline, in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Oremos + is an optional subscription with extra content.

FAQ

Questions?

Is Oremos free?

Yes. The Verse of the Day, the guided Rosary, the Daily Examen and confession prep are free, with no ads. Oremos + is an optional subscription with extra content, like more saints and reading plans.

Where is what I write for confession stored?

Only on your phone. The list is never sent online, it’s protected by your phone’s lock, and it’s deleted after you go to confession.

Do I need an account?

No. You can pray without one. An optional account lets you use Community and sync your Journal across devices.

Is it on Android and iPhone?

Yes: on Google Play for Android and the App Store for iPhone. On iPhone, it isn’t available in European Union countries yet.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The prayers, the Rosary and each day’s content work offline. Community and sync need a connection.

Which languages is it in?

English, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal). You can change it in Profile → Language.
